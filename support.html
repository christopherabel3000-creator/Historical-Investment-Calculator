What If I'd Invested, by Christopher Abel.

What If I'd Invested shows what money invested in the past would be worth today in five low-cost funds: an S&P 500 index fund, a Treasury bond fund, a gold fund, a bitcoin fund and a money market fund, or a mix of them. It can also test a retirement withdrawal plan against real history. It works fully with VoiceOver and other screen readers.

Get help or send feedback

Use the contact form for bug reports, questions and ideas, and say that it's about What If I'd Invested. You don't need an account. Add your email address if you'd like a reply.

Common questions

Where do the numbers come from?

Stock returns come from Robert Shiller's published dataset, bond and Treasury bill returns from Aswath Damodaran's datasets and the US Treasury, gold prices from the World Bank, and bitcoin prices from Coin Metrics. Inflation comes from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The calculator itself lists every source under About the numbers.

How current are the prices?

The calculator works with monthly average prices, and runs through the latest full month. New data comes out once a month, usually in the first couple of weeks. The app picks up new prices when you open it, and offers to switch to them if they arrive while it's open. Without an internet connection, it uses the latest prices it has.

Does it include fees, taxes and inflation?

Fund fees are included, based on real low-cost funds. Taxes are not. You can show any result in today's dollars, adjusted for inflation.

How does "Will my money last?" work?

It takes your withdrawals out every month and replays every real stretch of history the data covers, then builds 10,000 possible futures by shuffling real 12-month periods into new orders. It can tell you how often a plan ran out, or how much you'd need to save for it to last.

Is this investment advice?

No. It's a historical illustration for learning. Past returns don't predict future ones, and the calculator doesn't know your situation.

Does it have ads, purchases or tracking?

No ads, nothing to buy, no accounts and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
