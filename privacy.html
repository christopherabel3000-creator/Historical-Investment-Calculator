Privacy policy

What If I'd Invested, by Christopher Abel. Last updated November 17, 2026.

What If I'd Invested is a calculator that shows what past investments would be worth today, and how long savings would have lasted. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your name or any other personal information.

What you type stays on your device

Every calculation runs on your phone or computer. The amounts, dates and plans you enter are never sent anywhere and aren't saved after you close the calculator.

Price updates

The calculator's market prices are updated every month. In the iPhone app, the app downloads the latest prices when you open it, as a file from the calculator's website, and keeps a copy on your device so it works offline. That download sends nothing about you or your calculations. The website is hosted by GitHub, which, like any web host, receives your device's internet address when the page or the price file is loaded. GitHub handles it under its own privacy statement; the calculator doesn't keep or use it.

Children

The calculator isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the calculator ever handles information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your privacy, or anything else: use the contact form, and say that it's about What If I'd Invested. You don't need an account, and only the developer sees what you send. Add your email address only if you'd like a reply; it's used just to answer you. Messages are kept in Google Forms, under Google's terms.
